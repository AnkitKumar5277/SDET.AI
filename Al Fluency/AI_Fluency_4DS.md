### AI Fluency — Simple Hinglish

**AI Fluency** ka simple meaning hai: AI ke saath **effective, efficient, ethical aur safe** way mein kaam karna. Iske 4 main skills hain — **4 Ds**. 

### 4 Ds yaad rakhne ka shortcut:

**Ask → Say → Check → Sign**

1. **Delegation = Ask**
   Decide karo ki **AI ko kya kaam dena hai aur kya khud karna hai**.
   Example: 200 basic test cases AI se generate karwa sakte ho, but requirement ko samajhna khud karna chahiye. 

2. **Description = Say**
   AI ko **clear instructions** dena. Context, constraints aur expected output batana.
   ❌ "Login ke tests likho."
   ✅ Framework, fixtures, negative cases, naming convention etc. clearly batao. 

3. **Discernment = Check**
   AI ke answer ko **blindly trust mat karo**. Check karo ki answer actually correct hai ya nahi.
   AI ka answer confident aur professional lag sakta hai, phir bhi wrong ho sakta hai. 

4. **Diligence = Sign**
   AI ne kaam kiya ho, **responsibility tumhari hi rahegi**. Review karo, AI usage ke baare mein transparent raho aur final output ki responsibility lo. 

### 3 AI Modes

| Mode             | Simple Meaning                                          | Example                                        |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Automation**   | AI ko specific kaam do, AI execute kare                 | Selenium test ko Playwright mein convert karna |
| **Augmentation** | Tum + AI milkar kaam karo                               | AI ke saath test framework design karna        |
| **Agency**       | AI ko rules do, AI independently decisions/actions kare | AI agent nightly CI failures ko triage kare    |



**Important order:**
**Automation → Augmentation → Agency**. 

### QA ke liye sabse important point

AI se generated test **green/pass** ho gaya, iska matlab automatically ye nahi ki test good hai. Intentionally bug introduce karke check karo ki test **fail/red bhi hota hai ya nahi**. 

**One-line revision:**

> **Delegation = kya AI karega, Description = AI ko kaise samjhayenge, Discernment = output check karenge, Diligence = final responsibility hamari.**
